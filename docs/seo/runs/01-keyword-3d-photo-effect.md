## Content gaps (where Gifsy differentiates)

1. Interactive, not a video — no competitor page says "the visitor can drag it on your site".
2. Embed in Webflow / Framer / Squarespace — zero coverage.
3. Creation runs in the browser — Media.io/Fotor upload to servers. Must stay
   honest: photo stays local; free plan uploads intermediate activations.
4. How it actually works (depth map + subject matte + two displaced planes) with
   first-party screenshots — information gain nobody has.
5. Honest limitations (frame-filling subjects, busy backgrounds) — nobody says
   what fails.
6. One-time price vs credits/subscription everywhere else.
