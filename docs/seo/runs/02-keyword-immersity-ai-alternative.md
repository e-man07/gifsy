## Content gaps (where Gifsy differentiates)

1. **Nobody answers "which one gives me something interactive for my website?"**
   Every list is video/GIF exporters plus mis-filed mesh generators. Depthy is
   the lone interactive entry and it's unmaintained with no embed.
2. **Nobody states Immersity's actual current terms** (free = watermark +
   720p + non-commercial; $4.99+/mo; à-la-carte credits discontinued; homepage
   now sells displays). First-party screenshots of the pricing page dated
   2026-09 are cheap information gain.
3. **No output-type taxonomy.** A one-table split — *interactive embed* vs
   *MP4/GIF export* vs *3D mesh* vs *desktop cinemagraph* — resolves the
   confusion that makes G2/SourceForge/Animagen lists useless.
4. **No hands-on test.** Run the same photo through Immersity free, Gifsy,
   Depthy, Media.io, CapCut; show the results (GIF thumbnails + one live Gifsy
   embed). Nobody on the SERP has done this.
5. **Pricing model comparison.** Every alternative is credits or subscription;
   Gifsy is $9 one-time. Say it in the table, not just the copy.
6. **"Does it run in my browser?"** Immersity, Media.io, Animagen upload to
   servers. Gifsy runs depth + matte locally (honesty note: free plan uploads
   intermediate activations — see README backlog).
7. **Webflow/Framer/Squarespace path.** One paragraph + link to the embed
   guides (run 01 backlog) turns a comparison reader into the ICP.

## Ranking strategy

Greenfield. Worth pursuing: intent is commercial, the editorial competition is
small vendors with generic copy, and the query self-selects for people who
already understood "2D photo → depth animation" — which is Gifsy's product.

**Format:** match the SERP — a listicle *with* a head-to-head table — but lead
with the output-type split and a Gifsy-vs-Immersity comparison block. Do not
write a pure "Gifsy vs Immersity" page; the SERP is lists, and a one-product
page under-serves the query (Animate Photo AI's page is the weakest ranking
format in the set).

**Recommended slug:** `/alternatives/immersity-ai`
- Establishes a `/alternatives/` directory for later `/alternatives/media-io`,
  `/alternatives/leiapix` (redirect → immersity-ai; same product), etc.
- README backlog mentions `/compare/immersity-ai-alternative` — either works;
  pick one and keep `/compare/` for two-product "X vs Y" pages if ever needed.
- Add to `app/sitemap.ts` (priority 0.7, monthly).

**Word count target:** ~2,300–2,500 (average of the five editorial pages ≈
2,100 + 10%; directories excluded as non-comparable).

**Page structure (H1 → H2/H3):**

```
H1  Immersity AI (LeiaPix) Alternatives in 2026 — 8 Tools Compared by What They Actually Output
    Intro (≤100 words): name the tools, say we tested one photo through each,
    state the split: interactive embed vs video export vs 3D mesh.
H2  TL;DR — which Immersity alternative for which job (4-row verdict table)
H2  What changed with Immersity AI (and why people are leaving)
    LeiaPix → Immersity rename · free = watermark/720p/non-commercial ·
    $4.99–$99.99/mo credits · à-la-carte discontinued · homepage now sells
    displays · no embed. Screenshot of pricing page, dated.
H2  The three kinds of "3D photo" tools (don't compare across them)
    H3 Interactive embeds (Gifsy, Depthy)
    H3 Video / GIF exporters (Immersity, Media.io, Animagen, CapCut, Motionleap, DepthFlow)
    H3 3D mesh generators (Meshy, Tripo — different product; link out, don't list)
H2  Head-to-head table
    Columns: Tool · Output · Interactive on your site? · Free tier · Watermark ·
    Price model · Runs in browser? · Best for
H2  The 8 alternatives, tested with the same photo   (H3 each)
    1 Gifsy — interactive embed, $9 one-time, live demo embedded on page
    2 Depthy — free, interactive, unmaintained, GIF only
    3 Media.io 3D Image Maker — video, credits
    4 Animagen — GIF/MP4, $1/image
    5 CapCut 3D Zoom — free, mobile, video
    6 Motionleap — mobile, 2.5D video
    7 DepthFlow — open source, GPU, video
    8 Dzine (Stylar) — subscription, stylised
    Each: what you get · price · one honest limitation · sample output
H2  Gifsy vs Immersity AI, side by side   (two-column table + "choose Immersity if…")
H2  How to embed the result in Webflow / Framer / Squarespace  (short; links to guides)
H2  FAQ (8 Q, FAQPage JSON-LD)
    Is the LeiaPix converter still free? · Does Immersity AI watermark? ·
    What's the difference between Immersity AI and LeiaPix? · Can I embed an
    Immersity animation on my website? · Free alternative without watermark? ·
    Interactive vs video — which should I use? · Is Meshy an Immersity
    alternative? (no — meshes) · Does Gifsy upload my photo?
H2  Methodology + byline + "last tested" date
```

**Must-cover sections (from competitive read):** what-is-Immersity block,
free/paid flag per tool, comparison table with pricing, "how to choose", FAQ.

**Unique angle:** "compared by what they output" + the only interactive/embed
answer + first-party test of one photo.

**E-E-A-T required:** named author (open question in README), "tested on
2026-09-xx" line, screenshots of Immersity pricing and each tool's output,
visible Gifsy pricing, honest limitation per tool including Gifsy's (needs a
clear subject; free plan = 3 generations + badge).

**Internal links:** `/` (3D photo maker) · `/create` · `/pricing` · embed
guides (Webflow/Framer/Squarespace) · "how 3D photos work" explainer. Inbound:
link this page from `/pricing` ("compare to Immersity") and from the guides.

**Structured data:** `FAQPage` + `BreadcrumbList` + `ItemList` of the tools;
`SoftwareApplication` lives on `/`, not here.

**Snippet/AIO play:** put a 40–60-word direct answer under the H1 ("Immersity
AI (formerly LeiaPix) turns photos into depth-animated videos; its free plan is
watermarked and 720p. The closest alternatives are …") and keep the TL;DR table
as the first block after it — that's the cite-able unit.
